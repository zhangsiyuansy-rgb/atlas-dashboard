    第 1 轮 — 主问题
    Interviewer
    Tell me about a digital marketing campaign you led. What was your strategy, and how did you measure success?
    请讲一个你主导的数字营销campaign。你的策略是什么？你如何衡量成功？
    Candidate
    Last autumn I led a campaign for a home appliance brand to boost online sales before the holiday season. Our goal was to increase qualified traffic and lift conversion by 15 percent. I focused on short-form video with local creators and paired it with search ads for high-intent keywords. I owned the content brief, creator selection, and weekly budget shifts. We tracked click-through rate, add-to-cart rate, and cost per acquisition. Over six weeks, conversion rose 18 percent and CPA dropped 12 percent. The main lesson was to align creator content with search intent.
    去年秋天我主导了一个家电品牌的节前线上销售campaign。目标是提升有效流量并把转化率提高15%。我聚焦短视频与本地达人合作，同时投放高意图关键词搜索广告。我负责内容brief、达人筛选和每周预算调整。我们跟踪点击率、加购率和获客成本。六周内转化率提升18%，获客成本下降12%。最大的经验是让达人内容与搜索意图保持一致。

    第 2 轮 — 基于第一轮的追问
    Interviewer
    You mentioned creator content and search ads. How did you decide which creators to work with, and how did you measure their real impact?
    你提到达人内容和搜索广告。你如何决定与哪些达人合作？又如何衡量他们的真实效果？
    Candidate
    I built a shortlist based on audience overlap, past engagement quality, and content style fit. I avoided vanity metrics like follower count and looked at save and comment rates. We used unique links and promo codes for each creator to track traffic and sales. I also ran a holdout test in two regions to see the incremental lift. Creators with strong how-to content drove 30 percent more add-to-cart than entertainment-focused ones. That data helped us reallocate 40 percent of the budget to the top five creators in the second half of the campaign.
    我根据受众重合度、过往互动质量和内容风格匹配度筛选达人。我不看粉丝数这类虚荣指标，而是看收藏和评论率。我们为每位达人设置专属链接和优惠码来追踪流量与销售。我还在两个区域做了对照组测试来看增量。以教程类内容为主的达人带来的加购比娱乐类高30%。这些数据让我们在后半程把40%的预算集中到前五位达人。
