    今日面试问题 — Digital Marketing
    How would you design a digital marketing campaign for a new fitness app targeting Gen Z in China?
    你会如何为中国市场一款针对Z世代的新健身App设计数字营销活动？
    为什么问考察候选人对Z世代消费者行为、中国数字生态（小红书、抖音、微信）的理解，以及整合营销能力。
    真正想看能否提出具体渠道组合、内容策略、KOL合作及数据衡量方法，而非泛泛而谈。
    回答结构目标用户分析 -> 核心渠道选择 -> 内容策略(达人合作+UGC) -> 用户获取与留存 -> KPI设定
    避免照搬西方策略、忽略微信生态、用模糊词汇如'做一些推广'。
    三轮完整面试对话
    第 1 轮 — 主问题
    Interviewer
    How would you design a digital marketing campaign for a new fitness app targeting Gen Z in China?
    你会如何为中国市场一款针对Z世代的新健身App设计数字营销活动？
    Candidate
    First, I'd define the target audience: urban Gen Z aged 18-25, mostly students and young professionals, who value convenience, social recognition, and gamification. The core channels would be Douyin for short-video virality and Xiaohongshu for in-depth reviews and lifestyle integration. I'd launch a campaign titled '30-Day Fitness Challenge' with a branded hashtag. We'd partner with micro-KOLs (10-50k followers) who share real transformation stories. For user acquisition, we'd run in-feed ads on Douyin with interactive CTAs, and on Xiaohongshu, we'd seed KOC notes with discount codes. To drive retention, we'd integrate WeChat mini-program for community check-ins and share progress on Moments. Key KPIs: Cost per Install under 8 RMB, D1 retention 40%, and 10k UGC posts within the first month.
    首先，我会明确目标受众：18-25岁城市Z世代，主要是学生和年轻职场人，看重便捷、社交认可和游戏化。核心渠道选择抖音（短视频病毒传播）和小红书（深度测评与生活方式融合）。我会发起一个名为'30天健身挑战'的活动，并创建品牌话题标签。我们会与拥有1-5万粉丝的微KOL合作，分享真实的转变故事。在用户获取方面，我们在抖音上投放带互动行动号召的信息流广告，在小红书上铺KOC笔记并附上优惠码。为了留存，我们会集成微信小程序用于社区打卡，并鼓励在朋友圈分享进展。关键KPI：单次安装成本低于8元，次日留存40%，首月产生1万条UGC内容。
    回答结构分析
    回答具体，覆盖全链路，给出数字和平台策略，体现了对中国生态的理解。
    第 2 轮 — 基于第一轮的追问
    Interviewer
    That's a comprehensive plan. You mentioned micro-KOLs—how would you select and manage them?
    这是一个全面的计划。你提到了微KOL——你会如何选择和管理他们？
    Candidate
    For selection, I'd prioritize authentic fitness enthusiasts with high engagement rates (above 5%) rather than just follower count. We'd use a scoring matrix: relevance (fitness content ratio), engagement quality (comments sentiment), and audience demographics (18-25, urban). We'd start with 20-30 KOLs on trial, providing them with creative briefs but allowing flexibility. Management would involve a dedicated coordinator using a CRM tool to track deliverables, payment, and performance. We'd set up a private WeChat group for communication and feedback. To ensure authenticity, we'd avoid scripted content—instead, we'd offer free app membership and let them share genuine experiences. We'd also implement a referral bonus for KOLs who bring in high-quality leads.
    选择时，我会优先考虑互动率超过5%的真正健身爱好者，而不是只看粉丝数。我们会使用评分矩阵：相关性（健身内容占比）、互动质量（评论情感）和受众画像（18-25岁，城市）。我们会先与20-30位KOL试合作，提供创意简报但保留灵活性。管理方面，设专人用CRM工具跟踪交付、付款和表现。我们会建立微信私群进行沟通反馈。为确保真实性，不提供脚本，而是赠送免费会员让他们分享真实体验。我们还会为带来高质量线索的KOL设置推荐奖金。
    回答结构分析
    展示了量化选择和关系管理能力，强调真实性，符合Z世代偏好。
    第 3 轮 — 更深入的挑战性追问
    Interviewer
    Good. Now let's talk about measuring ROI. How would you attribute conversions across channels?
    好的。现在我们谈谈衡量ROI。你会如何跨渠道归因转化？
    Candidate
    Given the complexity of user journeys, I'd adopt a multi-touch attribution model. We'd use a combination of platform-specific tracking links (UTM parameters) and a unified analytics tool like Adjust or AppsFlyer. For Douyin, we'd leverage their native conversion API; for Xiaohongshu, we'd use deep links and coupon codes. Since WeChat ecosystem is walled, we'd set up a mini-program with event tracking. The attribution model would be time-decay (7-day window), giving more credit to touchpoints closer to conversion. We'd also run a holdout test: reserve 10% of the budget for a control group exposed only to organic content to measure lift. Key metrics: ROAS, CPA by channel, and assisted conversions. Weekly reporting with automated dashboards to quickly shift budget to top-performing channels.
    鉴于用户旅程的复杂性，我会采用多触点归因模型。我们会结合平台特定追踪链接（UTM参数）和统一分析工具如Adjust或AppsFlyer。对于抖音，利用其原生转化API；对于小红书，使用深度链接和优惠码。由于微信生态封闭，我们会设置带事件追踪的小程序。归因模型采用时间衰减（7天窗口），给靠近转化的触点更高权重。我们还会进行留存测试：预留10%预算用于仅接触自然内容的对照组，以衡量提升度。关键指标：ROAS、各渠道CPA和辅助转化。每周通过自动化仪表盘报告，快速将预算转移到表现最好的渠道。
    回答结构分析
    展示了高级归因知识，涉及具体工具和测试方法，体现数据驱动决策。
