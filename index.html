    第 1 轮 — 主问题
    Interviewer
    Tell me about a brand marketing campaign you led. What was the insight, and how did you measure success?
    请介绍一个你主导的品牌营销活动。洞察是什么，你如何衡量成功？
    Candidate
    I led a campaign for a skincare brand targeting young professionals. The insight was that they want effective products but feel overwhelmed by complex routines. We launched a three-step routine with simple packaging and short video tutorials. I worked with product, design, and KOL teams to roll it out across social and e-commerce. We measured success by engagement, add-to-cart rate, and repeat purchase. The campaign increased social engagement by 45 percent and repeat purchase by 18 percent in three months. The key lesson was to keep the message simple and consistent across all touchpoints.
    我主导了一个面向年轻职场人的护肤品牌营销活动。洞察是他们想要有效产品，但觉得护肤步骤太复杂。我们推出三步护肤routine，包装简洁，并制作短视频教程。我与产品、设计和KOL团队合作，在社交和电商渠道落地。我们用互动率、加购率和复购率衡量成功。活动使社交互动提升45%，三个月内复购提升18%。关键经验是信息要简单，并在所有触点上保持一致。

    第 2 轮 — 基于第一轮的追问
    Interviewer
    You mentioned repeat purchase increased. How did you isolate the campaign impact from other factors?
    你提到复购提升。你如何将活动影响与其他因素区分开？
    Candidate
    We used a holdout group in two cities where the campaign was not launched. We compared repeat purchase rates between the test and holdout groups over the same period. We also controlled for seasonality by looking at the same months last year. The lift in the test group was 18 percent, while the holdout group was flat. We also ran a short survey to ask new customers how they heard about us. About 60 percent mentioned the social tutorials. This gave us confidence that the campaign was the main driver, though we acknowledged other factors like pricing.
    我们在两个未上线活动的城市设置了对照组。我们对比同期测试组和对照组的复购率，并通过对比去年同月控制季节性因素。测试组提升18%，对照组持平。我们还做了简短问卷，询问新客如何知道我们。约60%提到社交教程。这让我们有信心认为活动是主要驱动，但也承认定价等因素的影响。
